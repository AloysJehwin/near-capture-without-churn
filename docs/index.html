What counts against the thesis

  1. A real loss cannot be ruled out. The interval reaches −18.9%. A loss of up to 18.9% is consistent with the data, so the strict form of the claim (no loss beyond 15%) fails.
  2. The gap was drifting down when the test had to stop. NEAR Intents ran +4.5% against its basket in the first 5 weeks and −7.0% in the last 6; −11.2% in the last three. Then Maya Protocol and THORChain report no volume from 2026-05-16.
  3. The basket follows NEAR Intents only loosely. Typical weekly gap before the switch: 0.14 log points. Pretending the switch happened on 2026-01-19, the test reports +6.4% where the truth is zero.
  4. One of three estimators cannot rule out break-even. The pooled-volume estimator gives +4.3% (−33.6% to +66.5%).
  5. Some window choices disagree. Of 21 combinations of event date and window, the break-even loss is ruled out in 18, not determined in 2 and not ruled out in 1. In 1 the whole interval is below zero.
  6. How much the cut rose depends on the window: 1.28x to 1.64x, which puts the break-even loss between 21.8% and 38.9%. As DefiLlama books the transfers, the capture ratio reads 1.64x; spread over the days the money was earned it is 1.48x.
  7. Volume did fall in dollars. NEAR Intents volume fell 18.4% after the switch. The peers together fell 17.2%.
  8. The later rise has no test of its own. The share of fees NEAR keeps went from 11.89% to 27.10% after the test window, with no dated change to test.
  9. The rules were written after a first look at the data. No out-of-sample test exists yet; the dated check below is the only one.
  10. Some partners did leave. Of the 10 largest before the switch, 4 sent less than half their earlier volume after it (zashi / zodl, infinex.near, rhea and solswap.intents-referral.near), and 2 of those less than 10% (infinex.near and rhea). In the latest window 1 is below 10% (infinex.near). The sample cannot say why a partner sent less.
  11. The largest partners sent less after the switch. 0.94x their earlier volume per day, with a sampling error of about 8% either way. NEAR Intents as a whole was 0.82x over the same weeks, so the partners did no worse than the total, but the total fell.

What would change the verdict

The verdict is computed by fixed rules. It turns to "not supported" if the take fails to rise under any window choice, if the interval reaches the break-even loss, if the estimate is below −15% and more than 50% of the clean runs are too, or if, in the latest 13 weeks, NEAR keeps less than 20% of fees or its share of the basket is more than 10 points below the level before the switch.

The last rule will be read again on the first snapshot with data through the day before 15 February 2027. Status today: not yet due.
